# Art the story asks for

Everything the story documents imply as a drawing, with what exists already and what each one is for.
Sizes assume the existing pipeline (`art/README.md`, `docs/instructions/art-tasks.md`).

**The recurring constraint is translation.** A drawn item costs one asset whatever the language count; a
written one costs a line per locale forever (`FORMAT.md`). At a dozen European languages that turns "draw
it" from a preference into the cheaper option, which is why several rows below are pictures of things that
could have been sentences.

## What this list does not include

Room props, tile art and the site-map renderer, which have their own briefs and their own queue
(`tile-art-brief.md`, `repaint-queue.md`). Nothing in the story asks for a new room.

## Open

1. **Do the ghosts get portraits, or are they a voice and a lit shape?** Five portraits is a real
   commitment; a consistent treatment that is not a face may be both cheaper and less frightening.
2. **Does the mosaic get rebuilt?** It is the ending either way. Abstract, it is a progress bar; narrative,
   every revealed slice is a story beat — and Part 3 has already done the design work.
3. **Are the two seals one asset with a difference, or two drawings?** One asset with a deliberate flaw is
   cheaper and makes the forgery findable by looking, which is what the arc wants.
