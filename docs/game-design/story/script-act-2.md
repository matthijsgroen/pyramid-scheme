# Act II — junior · what real looks like

Source copy, English only. Voice and rules: `cast.md`. 41 source lines.

**The act's job.** The player handles imitations for a whole tier without being told that is what they are,
so that Act IV's forgery is earned rather than sprung. And reading pays off for the first time — which is
the ending's mechanism arriving quietly, four tiers early.

**Fez is wrong about Thoth**, and Henut corrects him. The correction is what `mosaicJunior` already says.

## `junior_treasure_tomb` — Noble's Hidden Vault · Henut

`story.tomb.junior.*`

Ipi named her in Act I, with her father and the year of the debt, so the player arrives knowing exactly
who they are looking for.

Henut is vain, buried with imitations, and entirely certain they are originals. Her want is mundane and
real: **her name is spelled wrong on her own wall.** In Egyptian terms that is not vanity at all, and the
game does not say so — Fez does, badly, and is corrected.

> **Henut:** Finally. You will have come about the lapis. I assume Ipi is still going on about the lapis.
>
> **Explorer:** He is.
>
> **Henut:** It was glass. Beautiful glass. He knew.
>
> **Fez:** …Was the bracelet glass too?
>
> **Henut:** Everything here is the best imitation money could buy, young man, and I chose every piece of it
> myself.
>
> **Henut:** Now. The wall. Third sign along — that is not my name. Some apprentice with a chisel and no
> supervision.
>
> **Henut:** You have been learning your signs. I can tell. Fix it.

**The reading beat.** The player is asked to correct one glyph on Henut's wall — the first time collected
hieroglyphs do anything outside a tableau, and the mechanism the ending runs on.

> **Henut:** There. Now it will still be my name in a thousand years.
>
> **Explorer:** Ipi spelled it correctly.
>
> **Henut:** Ipi spelled everything correctly. It was his one redeeming feature.
>
> **Fez:** Does it matter, down here?
>
> **Henut:** It is the only thing that does.

**Fez, corrected:**

> **Fez:** She had a whole wall of Thoth up there. God of secrets.
>
> **Henut:** Thoth counts. Thoth writes down what you did and it stays written.
>
> **Henut:** Secrets are what people call arithmetic when they cannot do it.

**The introduction:**

> **Henut:** If you are going on — and you clearly are — there is a priest at Karnak who owes the dead a
> service he never performed. He will be insufferable about it. Priests are.
>
> **Henut:** …Have we met?
>
> **Fez:** I would remember.
>
> **Henut:** Yes. So would I.

**The act's tell** (`cast.md`): she almost places him and lets it go. It reads as a vain woman assuming
everyone has met her.
