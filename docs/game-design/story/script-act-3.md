# Act III — expert · the ask

Source copy, English only. Voice and rules: `cast.md`. 42 source lines.

**The act's job.** The world starts refusing the player — this is the tier of traps and armour — and the
refusal becomes a person. The priest states the terms of the offering, which is the arc's engine
(`arc-offering-to-the-gods.md`), and the ask is what hands over the waypoint (FORMAT §7: a waypoint comes
from the ask, never from the refusal).

**Fez is wrong about Amun-Ra**, and the priest corrects him — which is `mosaicExpert` word for word.

**Fez's bond note this act: he starts deferring.** He asks what the explorer thinks before saying what he
thinks, twice, and does not comment on it.

## `expert_treasure_tomb` — High Priest's Treasury · the priest

`story.tomb.expert.*`

He is still at his post and extremely particular about procedure. The rite was interrupted three thousand
years ago and he has not left off waiting for the rest of it.

> **Priest:** You are not the person I am expecting.
>
> **Explorer:** Who are you expecting?
>
> **Priest:** Somebody with the seal. It was to be brought here, set down there, and spoken over. None of
> those three things has happened.
>
> **Fez:** …How long have you been waiting?
>
> **Priest:** I do not keep count. It would be unprofessional.

**The ask** — this is the beat the waypoint hangs off:

> **Priest:** A proper offering is a thing a king was buried holding. Not a copy. Not a likeness. The thing
> itself, with his mark on it.
>
> **Priest:** Bring that and you will be announced. Bring anything else and you will not.
>
> **Explorer:** Announced to whom?
>
> **Priest:** You will know. Everybody does, eventually.

**Fez, corrected:**

> **Fez:** He is very taken with the sun, this one. All that Amun-Ra out front.
>
> **Priest:** Amun is the hidden one. Ra is the one anybody can see.
>
> **Priest:** They are the same god. That is not a coincidence, it is the entire idea.
>
> **Fez:** …Right. Yes. I did know that.
>
> **Explorer:** You did not.
>
> **Fez:** I did not.
