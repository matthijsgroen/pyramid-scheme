# Act IV — master · the thing itself, and the forgery

Source copy, English only. Voice and rules: `cast.md`. 44 source lines.

**The act's job.** The seal, the fake, and the turn. It works because of Act II: the player has handled
imitations for a whole tier without being told, so a convincing copy is convincing rather than cheap.

**The forgery is modern** (`arc-offering-to-the-gods.md`) — left by somebody recent, which is the twist
only available because the game is set now. The player's own map was bought secondhand, and that was
planted in Act I.

**Fez is wrong about Osiris**, and the pharaoh corrects him — `mosaicMaster` word for word.

**Fez's bond note: he is worried.** He suggests leaving twice and stays both times, and nobody remarks on
it.

## `master_treasure_tomb` — Pharaoh's Secret Hoard · the pharaoh

`story.tomb.master.*`

He has not fully accepted that anything has changed. His want is petty and specific, which is what keeps
him funny.

> **Pharaoh:** You may approach. You have approached. Very well.
>
> **Fez:** Your majesty.
>
> **Pharaoh:** Yes.
>

>
> **Pharaoh:** So does everyone. Mine has been taken twice and returned never.

> **Priest:** No.
>

>

> business here, and not long ago.

**The refusal points at nothing.** The waypoint came from the ask, back in Act III — the player was already
sent to `master_2`. This says only that what they hold is wrong.

## Open

   the hidden corridor is the point — but it makes him less useful than he is funny.
2. **Is the camp at `master_3` too much like a body?** It is deliberately kit and no person. The cough is
   the closest it comes and may be one detail too far.
3. **The refusal is the arc's best scene and it is four lines.** It may want to be six.
